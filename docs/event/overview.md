## Rules that can disqualify us

- **All work must be started during the event.** They disqualify several teams every year for this.
- Teams of 1–4; formed only by adding everyone on Devpost.
- Follow the MLH Code of Conduct.
- No personal hotspots/routers or anything that interferes with venue wifi.
- Some sponsor tracks have their own disqualifiers (e.g. Bright Data: mocked data, scraping behind logins). See [tracks/shortlist.md](../tracks/shortlist.md).

## Judging

- General criteria: **impact, quality, technical complexity, creativity.**
- Judges care more about the idea, effort, and thought process than polish, **but a live demo is required.**
- Prepare a ~2-minute pitch plus answers to likely questions.
- Sponsor tracks have separate criteria and sometimes extra deliverables (videos, READMEs, slides, registrations). See the shortlist.
